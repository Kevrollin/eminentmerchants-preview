# Asset manifest

Every image and brand asset used by the concept, where it came from, and what it may be used for.
Compiled 29 October 2026.

**Provenance.** Unless noted otherwise, all photographs are Eminent Merchants' own published content. They were downloaded from the business's YouTube channel ([@eminentmerchantsltd2865](https://www.youtube.com/@eminentmerchantsltd2865)) as public video thumbnails (`maxresdefault.jpg`) or auto-generated frames (`maxres1–3.jpg`) from `i.ytimg.com`.

**Status.** They are **suitable for a private concept shown to the owner only.** Before any public launch the owner must:

1. confirm permission to use them on the website, and
2. ideally supply the original photos, which are higher resolution than the ~1280×720 video frames.

No stock photography, AI-generated imagery or other dealers' photos are used. No people appear in any image used. Visible number plates were blurred.

## Locations

- `assets-src/eminent/…` holds the original downloaded source files. They are not deployed.
- `public/assets/eminent/…` holds the optimised WebP files the site serves, generated by `npm run images`.
- `scripts/images.config.mjs` holds the crop and blur settings for each image.
- `team/` and `backgrounds/` folders were **not created**. No team photos or background images are used: staff appear in many videos, but faces were avoided without consent.

## Replacing an image

1. Put the new original in `assets-src/eminent/<folder>/`.
2. Point the entry in `scripts/images.config.mjs` at it, and adjust or remove the `crop` and `blur` settings.
3. Run `npm run images`, then `npm run build`.
