# Eminent Merchants — research notes

Research for the website concept. Compiled 29 October 2026.
Internal document: not part of the customer-facing site.

## Summary

Eminent Merchants Ltd is a **used-car importer and dealer in Meru**. It trades publicly as *Eminent Merchants Motors* and *Eminent Merchants Car Importers LTD*. It runs an office and gravel yard on the **Meru–Maua Highway, just past Kenya Methodist University (KeMU) main campus, at Ruiri Junction**.

The business itself publishes this about what it does:

- sells ex-Japan stock from the yard
- imports vehicles to order for individual clients
- advertises local sourcing, buying and selling on behalf, asset financing, hire purchase and duty-exemption services

The phone and WhatsApp line is **0723 332 197** (+254 723 332 197).

"Car storage" appears only in the Google Maps listing name. No first-party source describes storage as a service, so the concept does not market it.

The most useful first-party source was the business's **YouTube channel**. It has 851 subscribers and 140 videos (counts on 29 Oct 2026), with stock tours, vehicle reviews and client handovers from June 2024 to April 2026. Its avatar is the business logo.

## 2. Reasonable design assumptions

These guide the concept but are not presented as business claims.

- **Brand palette.** The site uses deep navy, warm off-white and the logo red, with the logo blue for links. These are design choices adapted from the logo colours (red `#E72426`, blue `#2575BB`), not an existing brand guide.
- **Typeface.** Manrope (open-source) is a design choice. The logo uses its own condensed lettering, left unchanged.
- **Logo treatment.** The lockup was cropped from the round avatar and its white background removed. A white version was derived for dark backgrounds. These are working copies, not official artwork.
- **Import process steps** (tell us, review options, confirm and import, collect). Inferred from the "import to order" and "imported on behalf of client" posts. The owner must confirm the order, any deposit and paperwork, and how updates are shared.
- **Customer base.** The site addresses private buyers in Meru and across Kenya. A video titled "Happy Customer all the way from Nairobi" suggests clients come from outside Meru, but customer types are unconfirmed.
- **Vehicle focus.** Copy speaks of cars, SUVs and pickups (ex-Japan). Recent first-party content shows Toyota, Mazda, Honda, Suzuki, Subaru and Daihatsu cars, SUVs, pickups and small vans. The JCT profile also lists buses and trucks, but no recent first-party content shows them, so they are not marketed.
- **Recent-vehicle examples.** Shown as past work ("Sold" / "Imported for a client") with month and year. No prices are shown because the posted prices are out of date.
- **Imagery.** All photos are the business's own published images (YouTube thumbnails and auto-generated frames, about 1280×720). They are suitable for a private concept. The owner should supply originals for launch.

## 3. Details requiring owner confirmation

The same items appear as a checklist in [owner-checklist.md](owner-checklist.md).

1. Preferred trading name for the website: Eminent Merchants, Eminent Merchants Motors, or Eminent Merchants Car Importers.
2. **Car storage.** The Google Maps listing is named "Eminent Merchants Car storage". Is storage a commercial service, or should the listing name be corrected?
3. **Asset financing and hire purchase.** Who provides it, the eligibility rules, typical deposit and terms, and any regulatory wording needed. The site only says "ask which options are available".
4. **Duty exemption services.** Which buyers this applies to and what the business handles.
5. Sourcing markets (Japan, UK, Singapore, Thailand, South Africa): are all still active?
6. The real import process: steps, deposits, typical timelines, inspection, and how updates are shared.
7. Opening hours. **Not shown on the site;** visitors are asked to call ahead.
8. Official email address. None found; JCT hides it behind a login.
9. Exact map pin and directions wording. Confirm the Google Maps pin is the yard gate.
10. Whether the JCT address (1801 Gakurine, Kithoka, 60200) is still current or historical.
11. Preferred Facebook URL: `EminentMerchantsltd` or `eminentmerchantscarimportersltd`, and whether the website emcarsltd.com should be retired from the profile.
12. Current stock: models, years, specs, prices (if they may be published), availability and original photos.
13. Permission to use the photos and video frames on the website, and original high-resolution files.
14. Customer testimonials the business has permission to publish. None used.
15. Whether to name the contact person (Erick Kithinji Mbaeh) on the site.
16. Delivery claims. One listing (Mazda CX-5, Nov 2025) said "Free delivery anywhere in Kenya / Delivery within 2 days". Not used, because it was specific to that listing.
17. Any required legal disclaimers, for example on imported-vehicle condition, financing or pricing.

## Discrepancies and cautions

- **"Car storage" vs dealership.** Only the Maps listing name mentions storage. Every first-party source describes importing and dealing. The site follows the first-party evidence.
- **Address vs location.** JCT gives "1801 Gakurine, Kithoka, Meru, 60200". The business's own posts say "Meru–Maua Highway, just past KeMU main campus, at Ruiri Junction". The site uses the business's own wording; 60200 is Meru's postal code.
- **JCT profile text appears template-generated.** It contains leftover prompts ("Here are some additional details that you can include: >> The company's mission statement…"). Its claims ("major importer", "we investigate our vehicles before they are all imported", "an assortment of funding choices", "most competitive prices") are **not used** as copy.
- **emcarsltd.com** does not resolve. If it is still listed on Facebook, customers following it reach a dead link. This is a useful talking point for the pitch.
- **Superlatives in videos** ("Most Trusted Car Dealership in Kenya", "Best and cheapest…", "Save 355K–505K vs local yards") are **not used**.
- **Unrelated businesses.** Search results for "Eminent" and "Merchants" also returned unrelated companies (e.g. a UK "Magnate Merchants Ltd", "Eminent Air Thailand"). None were used.

## Positioning used

- **What they do:** sell ex-Japan cars from a yard in Meru, and import the exact car a client wants.
- **Why contact them:** a physical yard you can visit, stock and handovers shown openly on YouTube, one direct line for calls and WhatsApp.
- **How to enquire:** WhatsApp or call 0723 332 197, the enquiry composer (prefilled WhatsApp or SMS message), or directions to the yard.
